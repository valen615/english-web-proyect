Where the story begins

Our retelling of the opening of Re:Zero − Starting Life in a Different World, a Japanese isekai and dark-fantasy anime, adapted for our English storytelling assignment.

There was a guy named Subaru Natsuki. He doesn't live in a normal world — he lives in a place called Lugnica. He ended up there because one day, while he was out shopping, he suddenly felt light-headed. He closed his eyes for a moment, but when he opened them again, he was standing in a completely different world.

When he arrived in this new world, he gained a strange new ability called Return by Death. This ability means that whenever Subaru dies, he is pulled back in time to a specific checkpoint, with all of his memories intact. No one else can see or remember the loops — only Subaru knows they ever happened. To survive Lugnica, and to protect the people he meets there, he has to die again and again until he finally gets it right.
