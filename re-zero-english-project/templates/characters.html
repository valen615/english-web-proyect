A knight so skilled that most people consider him unbeatable in a fight. Calm, disciplined, and almost unnervingly polite, Reinhard fights to protect the kingdom and the people he's sworn to serve, and his sheer strength makes him one of the few characters Subaru can't out-plan his way around.

One of the Witch Cult's most dangerous Sin Archbishops, representing Gluttony. Lye feeds on people's names and memories rather than food, hiding underneath heavy robes, and stands as one of the story's most unsettling early threats to Subaru and his allies.

A watchful, eccentric guardian who appears later in the story, tasked with protecting a sanctuary that few people are allowed near. Once she decides someone is her "master," her loyalty becomes intense — and more than a little unsettling for whoever is on the receiving end of it.

Maid

She is a pink-haired demon (Oni) maid who works alongside her twin sister, Rem, at the Roswaal manor. Although she lost her powerful horn during a childhood attack—significantly weakening her magical abilities and stamina compared to her past—Ram remains fiercely proud, immensely confident, and highly intelligent.

Knight

Known as the "Finest of Knights," he serves Anastasia Hoshin, one of the candidates in the Royal Selection. Julius is distinguished by his refined appearance, polished manners, and strict adherence to chivalry, making him the personification of an ideal knight in the Kingdom of Lugunica.

Royal Candidate

Originally an orphan from Kararagi, she rose from poverty through her exceptional business acumen to become the head of the powerful Hoshin Trading Company. Recognizing her talents, she was nominated to represent Kararagi-style enterprise and commerce in Lugunica's Royal Selection.

Royal Candidate

She is a silver-haired Half-Elf who physically resembles the dreaded Witch of Envy, leading to widespread prejudice and fear from the public throughout her life.
