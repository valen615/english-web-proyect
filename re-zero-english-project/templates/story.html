The Story

This is the opening of the story we wrote for our English class assignment.

There was a guy named Subaru Natsuki. He doesn't live in a normal world — he lives in a place called Lugunica. He got there because one time he was out shopping and suddenly started feeling light-headed. He closed his eyes for a moment, but when he opened them up again, he was in a whole new world.

When he arrived in this new world, he gained a new ability called Return by Death. What this ability does is that when he dies, he gets sent back in time to a specific checkpoint.
