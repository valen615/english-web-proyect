About this project

What this site is, and where the story comes from.

This website was built for an English class assignment that asked us to research and present a story — real or fictional — in a creative format. We chose Re:Zero − Starting Life in a Different World, a Japanese anime and light novel series, and retold the opening of its story across these pages in our own words.

Every summary on this site is an original rewrite for the assignment, not a copy of the show's script or the novels' text. Character art and anime screenshots are copyrighted, so this project leaves clearly labeled spaces for them instead of including them directly — see the Gallery page for details.
